For 13 years I built and then led engineering at [WiziShop](https://wizishop.com), a multi-tenant e-commerce SaaS: from PHP developer to Head of Engineering, with a team that grew to nine. Along the way, **WiziBlocks**, a no-code Angular page builder (80+ blocks) that turned two-week pages into twenty-minute ones; a **generative AI platform** wired into the merchant admin, about twenty tools shipped in a few months; and the long, unglamorous modernization of a legacy PHP stack toward CQRS and DDD. I care more about teams and systems that last than about anything that demos well.

**What's next:** I'm looking for a CTO or Engineering Manager role, full remote or around Nice / Sophia Antipolis. Ideally a team that builds AI-native, where the engineering culture is still being written.

My open-source work right now is [**Atlas Mind**](https://github.com/Anakior/atlas-mind): a git-native knowledge base served over MCP, so humans and AI agents read and write the same versioned memory. Zero-dependency Python server on PyPI, TypeScript client.

I also train developers in just about any language, because I like learning and handing it over in equal measure.

Off the clock I make games (**TheEnd**, a colony sim aboard humanity's last ark, with cosmic horror leaking in, in C# / MonoGame) and write dark fantasy I'm in no hurry to finish. The rest of the time I'm an RPG player through and through, and I take their systems apart like an engineer. I'd sooner read the raw numbers than trust a guide, the same way I don't trust an LLM that invents game mechanics with a straight face.

Happy to talk SaaS architecture, leading engineering teams, AI in production, or which RPG is worth far too many hours.
